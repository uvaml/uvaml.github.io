**Overview**
Continuing the success from the AI/ML resource in the past two years (see photos and posters in [2024](https://virginia.box.com/s/uf515cq0bi1lm2echaexfv02dj66643b) and [2025](https://virginia.box.com/s/ujx9ilkk4wm0rfalwjzp7sy5kzdj2kgp), we will have our third UVA AI/ML resource fair at Link Lab again this year! 

The goal of the resource fair is to encourage collaboration between labs and graduate students, and for motivating undergrads to look for research opportunities. This resource fair includes posters from various labs across departments in SEAS.
